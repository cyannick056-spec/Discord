# Salas neutras de Shis Stream

Fondos creados con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Se conservó el fondo original. Cada opción tiene CRT y TV plana en horizontal y vertical. Las preferencias se guardan como `roomTheme` y los cristales medidos están en `public/room-theme-glass.mjs`.

Se carga solo el ambiente elegido; no se aplican luces ni filtros de color sobre figuritas. La lluvia de estos fondos es fotográfica, sin animación adicional.

## Prompts y archivos

### midnight-crt-wide.webp

Ruta: public/rooms/themes/midnight-crt-wide.webp

Use case: precise-object-edit / photorealistic-natural. Asset type: background for an interactive television room. Edit the reference into this environment: Quiet living room at 3 am, charcoal grey plaster walls, muted dark walnut furniture, dark grey sofa, a dim soft warm-white lamp on right (not orange), window on left lit only by faint neutral moonlight. Cozy, readable furniture details, low saturation, no blue or amber light.
Keep the entire camera framing, furniture geometry, TV model and size, TV black glass silhouette and EXACT screen position pixel aligned with the reference. Preserve foreground sofa/table positions and depth, and keep all usable surfaces clear for user decorations. Only change the room materials, wall decor, weather, light and mood. TV glass must remain empty, very dark, with no picture, static, text, glare or reflections. Do not move or redesign the TV or its black glass, or resize its bezels. Keep the same image aspect ratio: 16:9 landscape. Do not add people, figurines, game consoles, UI, typography, logos, watermarks, collages or labels. This is a distinct full room photograph, not a global color filter; natural materials and white lighting with subtle true texture.
