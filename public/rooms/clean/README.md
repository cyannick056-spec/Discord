# Neutral rooms

The television is rendered separately with one shared SVG per model.

## midnight wide

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a clean background for an interactive TV room, 16:9 horizontal landscape.
A quiet modern living room at 3 am. Smooth charcoal-grey walls, a matte dark-grey low cabinet with simple closed drawers, dark ash wood floor, grey cloth sofa. Subtle neutral moonlight from a side window, a small dim warm-white lamp. Dark but readable, no vivid blue or amber lighting.
Brand new room, not a variant of the previous wooden Japanese living room. Realistic clean furniture materials, restrained low-saturation palette, no synthetic grunge, no sparkles, no exaggerated grain or texture. View straight on from a seated viewer at the sofa, cabinet front parallel to camera with no perspective skew. A broad low cabinet is centered against an unobstructed plain wall; its EMPTY top surface forms a straight horizontal line exactly at 62 percent of image height and spans the middle 82 percent of the image width. Reserve the entire wall above the cabinet in the middle 60 percent of width as clear empty wall. Television will be added by code: do NOT draw any television, monitor, screen, dark screen placeholder, frame, electronics, figurines, consoles, clutter or people. A simple coffee table may be below 78 percent of height, subtle sofa edge at the bottom below 94 percent. Window only in left outer 15 percent and lamp only in right outer 12 percent; do not overlap the central empty TV area. Natural interior photography, eye-level, believable matte material finishes. No text, logos, watermarks, UI or collage.
