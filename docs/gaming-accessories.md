# Gaming accessories

Six independent photographic props, made with built-in image generation. Transparent RGBA originals were inspected; WebP conversion and proportional downsampling to a maximum of 768 pixels are export preparation only. No painted TV reflection or external glow is included. Existing room light grading, practical lights, TV illumination and contact shadows apply at runtime.

Presets contain different arrangements of floor discs, open cases, consoles, cartridges and shelf figures. Each object is individually movable, rotatable, removable and saved only in the active view. Presets use authored support alignment through the editor; they do not replace other saved views or personal objects. Applying a preset again reuses its objects.
