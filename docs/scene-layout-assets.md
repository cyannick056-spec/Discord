## Prompts

### Mueble negro

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: one isolated photographic furniture sprite for a realistic room editor. Primary request: a low wide matte-black TV media cabinet with two doors, slim short feet and a dark walnut top, empty top. True transparent background. Front-facing symmetrical view with only a small elevation (about 8 degrees) revealing the flat top surface, no yaw, no dramatic perspective. Entire object fully visible and centered, occupying about 90% of image width. Natural wood grain and realistic materials, neutral soft daylight, no colored illumination. No floor, no room, no drop shadow outside the furniture, no glow or baked reflections, no text, logos, TV, controllers, plants or other objects. Photographic cutout with clean alpha edges, landscape canvas. The unobstructed flat top must be suitable for placing a separate television.
