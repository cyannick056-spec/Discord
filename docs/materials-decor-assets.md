# Materiales, decoración y lluvia cercana

## Materiales

Roble, nogal, caoba, lacado blanco, negro mate, lacado rosa, acero cepillado, mármol y piedra gris, además del material original. Se aplican a la tapa o al mueble entero con color, intensidad, escala de textura y rugosidad. Los reflejos responden al acabado elegido.

## Edición de ambos fondos de lluvia

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: photographic room background for interactive TV lighting. Edit target: the supplied room photo. Change ONLY these two surface details: remove all fixed raindrops and water streaks from the window glass while keeping the softly blurred cloudy garden behind it, because animated droplets will be rendered in real time; remove the bright reflective/window-light patch on the wooden floor and make the entire floor uniformly matte dark walnut with subtle diffuse illumination and visible natural grain. Preserve EXACTLY the framing, dimensions, window frame position, two panes, wall texture, colors and all architecture. No furniture, no TV, no glow, no additional objects. Keep photographic realism.
