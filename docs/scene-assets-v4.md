Generados con la herramienta integrada de imágenes. Los PNG originales se conservaron; las copias WebP de producción preservan la transparencia y la resolución. La TV usa su material negro original por defecto y el color personalizado se limita a su alfa.

Entire television and single central pedestal included, thick matte BLACK ABS bezel, subtle black speaker grille across bottom, a tiny unobtrusive power button, square clean outer corners with very slight rounding, rectangular black glass display EXACTLY 16:9. Single short central rectangular black neck and low oval black pedestal base. Honest product photograph, no bloom, no soft focus, no chrome, no silver, no white highlights, no bright rim, no image filters, no logo or text. Flat even soft illumination reveals black plastic without making it gray or white. Clean precise cutout; no room, floor or shadows outside product.
